New here?

What to expect in a first meeting

This guide is a fictional example of what first-meeting guidance can look like.

Nobody is expected to know anything. Newcomers are welcome, and there is no requirement to register in advance for this sample.

Arrival

How to attend

  • Come a little early and find a seat.
  • Introduce yourself when you are ready.
  • Silence, listening, and presence are always valid forms of participation.

Session flow

What happens there

  1. 01

    You will be welcomed and sit with a small group.

  2. 02

    The group spends a short period in guided meditation.

  3. 03

    A reading or text is used to frame discussion.

  4. 04

    There is open time to share if you want to.
