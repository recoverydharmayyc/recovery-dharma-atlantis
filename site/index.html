What this meeting is

Recovery Dharma is a peer-led meeting format with structured time for silence, reading, meditation, and sharing. This fictional Atlantis page uses that style in a simplified, first-screen-first structure.

The goal is steady, practical support: a clear invitation, predictable weekly meeting details, and respectful expectations for first-time visitors.

What a newcomer can expect

  • A short opening and a structured meeting flow.
  • A low-pressure atmosphere with peer-led guidance.
  • Option to listen first and speak only when and if you choose to.
  • Clear expectations about time, format, and meeting location.
